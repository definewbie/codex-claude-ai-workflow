Agency emerges when reasoning is coupled with action. Reasoning is the
internal process of planning or deciding the next step. Action extends this
by invoking tools, retrieving data, executing code, or calling external
services. Together they form an iterative loop: reason, act, receive feedback,
and adjust. This loop is the foundation of agentic behavior.
