This modular approach makes it easy to compose increasingly capable
MASs step-by-step: first by wiring up specialized workers, then by
combining entire teams into larger graphs. In practice, this means you can
start small—say with search, scraping, and patent lookup in a research
team, and then plug that into a writing team to form a full end-to-end
pipeline, or extend it even further with internal data and chart writing and
plotting agents.

To move from individual teams to a proper hierarchy, you’ll need to build
another graph that ties everything together. Think of this as your “super
graph,” shown in Example 2-29.
