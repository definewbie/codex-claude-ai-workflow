Deus Ex Machina: What Is Possible Right
Now?

Once you view agents as HSMs with states, the autonomy spectrum
becomes a matter of how much choice you place inside a superstate.
Routers are one-edge FSMs with a single guard. Tool-calling agents are
HSMs with a WORKING superstate that loops among PLAN, ACT, and
REFLECT. MASs compose multiple subgraphs under a supervisor
superstate, sometimes with parallel regions. The system still runs inside
explicit states and guards; perceived “autonomy” is just a richer choice
inside well-bounded regions.

In ancient Greek theater, a deus ex machina was the sudden appearance of a
god to resolve an unsolvable conflict or problem. It’s tempting to imagine
AI agents in the same way: a machine that suddenly acts with full autonomy
and intelligence solving all your problems. But that’s not what’s possible
today; agents are engineered systems. Their strength comes not from hidden
