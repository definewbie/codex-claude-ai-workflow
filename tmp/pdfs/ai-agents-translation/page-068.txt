The process begins at __start__; this hands control to the agent, and
then branches depending on the outcome:

           If the agent decides it has reached a conclusion, it produces an end
         signal and moves to __end__.

           If more reasoning or action is required, the agent issues a continue
         signal, possibly calling tools to gather additional information or
        perform actions.
