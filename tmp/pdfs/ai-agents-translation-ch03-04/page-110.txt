  WHO’S TEACHING WHAT AND HOW: PPO VERSUS DPO
           VERSUS GRPO VERSUS GSPO

If you’re wondering which common RL methods actually require an extra
reward or judge model, Table 3-3 makes this clear. Proximal policy
optimization (PPO), as used in reinforcement learning from human feedback
(RLHF), combines a learned reward model with a critic for stability and
variance reduction. Direct preference optimization (DPO) removes both,
optimizing directly on pairwise preferences with an explicit KL to a reference.
GRPO follows the PPO idea but replaces absolute rewards with relative
rankings across groups, removing the critic while keeping ratio clipping and
KL regularization. GSPO extends GRPO to sequence-level optimization,
which improves stability on long or complex outputs.
