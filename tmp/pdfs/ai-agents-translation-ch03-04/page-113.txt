This group-based normalization stabilizes learning by anchoring each update
within its local context. As you learned in “Learning on a Leash: KL Divergence”,
the KL divergence puts a leash on the policy, so that improvement doesn’t lead to
instability via too much divergence from the reference.

In practice, GRPO operates at the token level. The clipping and KL penalty are
applied incrementally as the model generates each token, allowing the training
signal to remain both adaptive and efficient.
