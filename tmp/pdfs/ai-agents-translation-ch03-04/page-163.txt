So, even though encoder-only models once seemed overshadowed by the
rise of decoder-only architectures, their evolution has quietly continued.
Modern variants such as NomicBERT, ModernBERT,5 and NeoBERT
integrate SOTA techniques like rotary positional encodings for extended
context windows and FlashAttention kernels for faster training and
inference.
