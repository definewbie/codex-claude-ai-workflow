When you collect multiple trajectories, you get rollouts. Each rollout is one
complete story of reasoning. Some reach the goal, some fail, but all of them teach
the agent something about how its own decisions lead to different outcomes. RL
turns these experiences into structure, by turning raw sequences of text into
measurable cause and effect. Table 3-2 gives you a conceptual compass of these
concepts before we start looking into how you can apply them.
