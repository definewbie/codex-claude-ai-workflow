GSPO

GSPO takes the principles of GRPO further by refining how the model interprets
and optimizes its experience. While GRPO evaluates reasoning at the token level,
GSPO moves one level higher and optimizes entire sequences as units. Figure 3-4
shows a high-level overview of the GSPO mechanics.





  Figure 3-4. Unlike GRPO, which evaluates at the token level, GSPO computes rewards and KL divergence
                                      over complete sequences.

The motivation behind GSPO is straightforward: the reward belongs to the entire
sequence, not to individual tokens. If the model receives feedback for the whole
response, then the optimization should also act at that scale. By shifting the
learning signal from tokens to sequences, GSPO aligns the unit of optimization
with the unit of reward.
As before, the model starts from a query q and a set of responses {oi}Gi=1 sampled
from the old policy πθold. But now, instead of token-level likelihood ratios, GSPO
measures how much the new policy diverges from the old one across the entire
response. This sequence-level importance ratio is defined as:

                                        1/|oi|
              πθ(oi ∣q)               1         |oi|       πθ(oi,t ∣q, oi,<t)
   si(θ) =           = exp             log             ∑ t=1      ( πθold(oi ∣q) )         [ |oi|               πθold(oi,t ∣q, oi,<t) ]

where |oi| denotes the number of tokens in the response. This length normalization
keeps the ratio numerically stable, preventing longer outputs from producing
disproportionately large updates.
