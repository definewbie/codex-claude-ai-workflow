This shift from token-level to sequence-level optimization has a profound effect. It
removes the instability that can emerge when token-level corrections introduce
noise, especially in long responses or very large models. By clipping and
rewarding entire responses instead of individual tokens, GSPO makes learning
smoother, more reliable, and better aligned with how rewards are assigned in
practice.

It also simplifies reinforcement learning infrastructure. Because GSPO operates on
sequence likelihoods, it can often reuse probabilities already computed during
inference rather than recomputing token-level likelihoods during training. This
makes it both computationally efficient and stable across large-scale systems.
