At test time, the model’s reasoning budget is spent running this loop: selecting,
expanding, simulating, and backpropagating. The LLM generates candidate
answers, evaluates them, and uses those evaluations to guide the next move. This
process lets the model explore more possibilities than simple one-pass decoding,
producing solutions that are both higher in quality and more explainable.

Adaptive Branching Monte Carlo Tree Search (AB-MCTS)
Adaptive Branching Monte Carlo Tree Search (AB-MCTS)4 extends standard
MCTS by dynamically deciding whether to explore new branches or to refine
existing ones. This adaptive decision process represents the classic exploration–
exploitation trade-off in RL.

This flexibility is essential for your agents, because even a single prompt can
generate an infinite variety of responses. A fixed branching factor, as used in
standard MCTS, limits this potential. AB-MCTS overcomes that by allowing the
