Usage: dualctl delivery finalize [-hV] --commit=<commit> [--workdir=<workdir>]
                                 <workstreamId>
Verify and record the commit for an applied delivery candidate.
      <workstreamId>
      --commit=<commit>     Commit SHA created in the original repository.
  -h, --help                Show this help message and exit.
  -V, --version             Print version information and exit.
      --workdir=<workdir>   Source worktree directory. Defaults to current
                              directory.
